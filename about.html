Who We Are

ABOUT MAB WONDERLUST SAFARIS

Mab Wonderlust Safaris is a Kenyan tourism company committed to creating meaningful travel experiences that connect people with Kenya, Africa and the wider world.

We combine the best of wildlife, nature, culture, heritage, adventure and human connection to craft journeys that go beyond the ordinary.
