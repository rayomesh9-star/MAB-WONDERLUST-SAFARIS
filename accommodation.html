Accommodation

COMFORT IN NATURE. LUXURY IN THE WILD.

From luxury lodges to tented camps, each stay is chosen to immerse you in the landscape while providing all the comfort you need after a day in the bush.

Luxury Lodges

Premium accommodation with stunning views, fine dining, and world-class service in Kenya's most exclusive settings.

Tented Camps

Traditional safari tents with modern amenities, offering an immersive wilderness experience under star-filled skies.

Eco-Lodges

Sustainable lodges built with low environmental impact, supporting conservation and local communities.

Family Accommodation

Family-friendly lodges and camps with dedicated kids' programs, spacious rooms, and safe environments for young travelers.

Conservancy Retreats

Exclusive stays within private conservancies for intimate wildlife encounters and personalized service.

Beach & Coastal

Oceanfront resorts and lodges along Kenya's stunning coastline, perfect for unwinding after your safari adventure.
